**Patch 1 loader choice.** The worker keeps the E3 class and options, `Qwen3VLForConditionalGeneration` with the embedding read from `model.model(...).last_hidden_state`, which is the same backbone path as the official `Qwen3VLForEmbedding`. ## Question

EXP-038 selected C2. Nearly all of its gain came from E3 (Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B, frozen, one neutral instruction, `max_pixels` 262,144). E3 alone matched the three-encoder Fixed Ridge on P2 test-like Contrast. **Hypothesis:** the VLM-embedding image representation has not saturated. A larger checkpoint, a pool of neutral instructions, or a higher input resolution each give a better image-level estimate of the deviation term, and passed through the unchanged C2 recipe they raise test-like Contrast over C2.

Out of scope: language-conditioned prompting (E4 was a clean negative), new losses or heads, fine-tuning, Development tuning, learned stacking, constant retuning, CS-CVA training, external data.

## Candidates and gate

Each candidate applies the EXP-038 neighborhood correction with unchanged constants. The similarity space covers every image encoder feeding the candidate, including Fixed Ridge's.

- **D1, D2, D3** = round6(0.5 × Fixed Ridge + 0.5 × mean(E1, E2, E5 / E6 / E7)).
- **D4** = round6((Fixed Ridge + round6(mean(E1, E2)) + round6(mean(E3 and every available E5/E6/E7))) / 3). D4 is unavailable only when none of E5–E7 completed.
- **Unavailable candidates:** a candidate whose arm failed or was skipped is `NOT_AVAILABLE`, with its reason.

The gate compares each candidate with **C2 recomputed in this run** from the sealed features, never with the archived CSV. All of the following are required:

1. Filtered Contrast Δ ≥ +0.003.
2. Painting-paired 95% bootstrap lower bound > 0 (20,000 resamples, seed 203904).
3. At least 4/5 filtered fold wins.
4. Positive gain after trimming the 5% largest absolute painting deltas.
5. No full-P2 Contrast regression.
6. Macro Log Loss regression ≤ +0.005 bits in both scopes.

**Selection:** among passing candidates, the highest filtered Contrast wins. Within 0.001, the simpler candidate wins (D2 = D3 < D1 < D4). A D2/D3 tie goes to the lower measured GPU extraction time. If nothing passes, the result is NO_GO.

**Diagnostics (not gating):** standalone P2 metrics for E5–E7; E5 − E3, E6 − E3 and E7 − E3 paired intervals; centered residual correlation against Fixed Ridge and against E3; the decomposition table; per-language and per-painting deltas; leave-one-fold-out gains.

### Budget and forecast method

- **Allowance:** 10.5 h including setup, with a 20-minute persistence reserve.
- **Throughput:** Benchmark runs two passes over 32 seeded real items per new arm, on that arm's GPU placement. The warm second pass gives items/s.
- **Extraction forecast:** items / rate + measured load + a 120 s download allowance. E6 runs 3 extra instructions per image (4,968 items).
- **CPU forecast:** 2.5 × the measured on-platform C2 replay time + 600 s; export is allowed 600 s.
- **Margin:** the total is multiplied by 1.25. If it exceeds the remaining time, E5 is skipped first, then E7.
- **Planning figures:** EXP-038 measured E3 at 9.0 images/s (`max_pixels` 262,144) and E4 at 8.9 items/s on one T4. These are planning figures only, not EXP-039 measurements.
