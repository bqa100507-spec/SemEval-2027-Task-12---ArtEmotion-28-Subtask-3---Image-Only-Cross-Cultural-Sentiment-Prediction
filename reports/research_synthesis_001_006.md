# ST3 research synthesis, EXP-001 through EXP-006

The project tests which changes to a frozen-vision system improve within-painting sentiment prediction across target languages. The findings below use the pinned ArtEmotion-28 release. Original training paintings supply the five shared grouped folds. The public development split is separate from fitting and rank selection. Contrast is the official primary metric. Macro Log Loss in bits measures the quality of the predicted probabilities. [Organizer evaluation rules](https://github.com/artemotion-28/artemotion-28/blob/cf7cc444cf56617f3b8417497c6930ad172c5c99/README.md#evaluation)

## What the evidence supports

**The strongest controlled result is the effect of image–language interactions within this system.** The B-to-C comparison held frozen CLIP, eligible pairs, fitting and scoring fixed while adding the interaction block. C consistently beat B, and incorrect image assignment reduced C's scores. The comparison supports reporting an image-dependent interaction effect for this corpus. The language prior already captures substantial Contrast, and image shuffling changes the inputs to a fitted model. Neither observation identifies a causal cultural mechanism. Per-language scores describe annotation groups with uneven coverage and often few votes per pair.

**The alternatives identify trade-offs.** Direct factorization was less reliable than C under the tested regularization and budget. Post-hoc SVD is a plausible storage compression diagnostic with different training requirements. Small auxiliary losses gave limited gains on these folds. DINOv2 regressed on both metrics even at matched head width. SigLIP 2 had a small positive signal and lower loss, with a larger head and mixed painting-level effects. These are results for specific checkpoints and protocols. The experiments do not isolate image-text versus self-supervised training as a cause.

## Reused folds and claim boundaries

The five folds preserve painting isolation, but the project examined them repeatedly and later hypotheses were informed by earlier results. Hyperparameters and promotion thresholds fixed within a study constrain within-study tuning. They cannot undo selection of research directions across studies. Fold estimates also share training paintings, and the public development split has been used for earlier evaluation. Fold dispersion and the EXP-002 paired bootstrap describe variation under the observed data and fits. They do not establish independent replication or account for all adaptive comparisons. The SigLIP 2 near miss stays a near miss. Retuning the gate or treating a new split of the already examined paintings as untouched confirmation would weaken the evidence.

## Paper contribution and next direction

A defensible system paper can describe the reproduced benchmark, a controlled explanation of why the full interaction head helps, and the conditions under which compression, objective changes and representation swaps improved or regressed. The contribution would be the evidence and reproducible protocol. Claims about a new architecture or generalization require separate support.

**Next research direction.** Preregister one paired replication of the full-interaction C versus shared-weight B comparison on genuinely untouched paintings through an organizer-permitted blind protocol. Freeze both models, input rules, primary Contrast comparison and uncertainty procedure before seeing outcomes. If fresh feedback is unavailable, present EXP-002 as strong within-corpus evidence and keep independent replication open. Retain C-CLIP as the current system.
