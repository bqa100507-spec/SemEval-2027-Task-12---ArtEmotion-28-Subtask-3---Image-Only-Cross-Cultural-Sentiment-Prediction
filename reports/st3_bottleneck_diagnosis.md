# ST3 bottleneck diagnosis

**Keep Corrected Ridge as the competition candidate. No further fine-tuning is supported by this audit.** Language-specific image effects are the main measured source of Contrast gain over the language prior. The largest remaining ranking errors occur where the observed language differences are small and vote panels are sparse. Residual errors also vary across languages and artwork styles. These data leave room for better interactions, but they give no evidence that a CLIP-L adapter would recover enough of that error to beat Corrected Ridge.

## Vote reliability and residual error

- **Sparse panels.** P2 contains 69,719 eligible votes, with 12,279 of 13,600 panels (90.3%) at five votes and 42.7% unanimous. Average observed variance across a painting's language shares is 0.04845 full and 0.05006 test-like. The estimated sampling components are 0.02819 and 0.02841, or **58.2% and 56.7%**. The calculation uses the mean `y(1−y)/(n−1)` per painting and includes paintings with constant observed shares. The existing verified R1 vote split (2 versus 3 sampled rows) gave Pearson **0.232** [0.216, 0.248] full and **0.242** [0.225, 0.260] test-like among draws with two nonconstant panels. Only 30.5% and 31.0% of pair orders were untied in both panels. The variance calculation assumes independent binomial votes and uncorrelated cross-language sampling errors. The release lacks rater IDs, and both split panels use the same collected population. These are descriptive noise estimates with no established performance ceiling or assignment of model error to sampling noise.
- **Ordering and probability quality.** Test-like painting-balanced order agreement rose from **65.48%** for the prior to **65.83%** for additive, **70.62%** for interaction and **70.82%** for Corrected Ridge. Corrected Ridge agrees on **63.31%** of language orders with observed differences up to 0.20 and **79.59%** where the difference is at least 0.40. Observed ties are excluded and prediction ties receive half credit. Painting-centered MSE falls from **0.03842** additive to **0.03253** Corrected Ridge. Its exact mean per-painting MSE decomposition is **0.01999** mean-level discrepancy, **0.01372** spread discrepancy and **0.01881** correlation discrepancy. These are descriptive components, with the correlation term also scaled by both spreads. Shared visual sentiment mostly helps Log Loss. A positive affine adjustment within a painting preserves Pearson, so static recalibration alone has no demonstrated Contrast benefit.
- **Where errors concentrate.** On eligible test-like paintings where the estimated sampling component meets or exceeds observed variance, mean Contrast is **0.3225** (410 paintings), versus **0.5316** elsewhere (842). This ratio-based split uses the same noisy observations and can mechanically select smaller observed spreads. Five-vote pairs have higher estimated sampling variance than larger panels (0.02865 vs 0.01922), while larger panels have higher observed MSE (0.05887 vs 0.05122), consistent with differing case mix. Kyrgyz has centered MSE **0.04716** with sampling variance **0.02161**. Yoruba has **0.03812** with **0.03648** estimated noise, and Hindi **0.02572** with **0.02259** estimated noise. Centering changes sampling variance, so these columns should be compared descriptively. Impressionism scores **0.4369** (231 paintings) versus **0.4878** for Expressionism (99), despite lower estimated sampling noise. Style is audit metadata. Neither descriptive slice isolates a causal representation failure.

## One next action

**Freeze the verified Corrected Ridge release as the evaluation candidate, at zero GPU cost.** The bounded audit gives clear evidence for the interaction the incumbent already models. EXP-004's strongest tested metric-aligned objective added only +0.001834 in its original fold means. EXP-014's CLIP-L tail LoRA delivered +0.000891 in fusion on its own protocol. EXP-030/031 saw held-out Contrast deteriorate even while surrogate losses improved. The recent Qwen QLoRA pilot improved over its frozen control yet trailed Corrected Ridge by 0.0970 on test-like fold 3. Its related semantic and concept sprints yielded +0.002687 and −0.006065 over Corrected Ridge, respectively. Sparse labels, remaining interaction errors and repeated P2 selection are credible constraints. The available comparisons cannot rank representation quality against optimization as causal explanations. A new CLIP-L adapter would revisit mechanisms with weak observed gains on the same vote panels.
